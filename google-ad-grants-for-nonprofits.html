Google Ad Grants
for nonprofits,
applied for, set up and managed.

Eligible nonprofits can receive up to $10,000 a month in Google Search advertising. Loveland Brand Design handles the application, the setup and the monthly work that keeps the grant active.

The Program

A grant
of Search ads.

Google Ad Grants gives eligible nonprofits up to $10,000 a month in Search advertising on Google.com. Your ad appears when someone searches for what your organization does. That puts your mission in front of donors, volunteers and the people you serve at the moment they look.

The $10,000 is a monthly ceiling. Actual spend depends on your keywords, bids, ad quality and how many people search.

What the Grant Covers
  • Search ads shown on Google.com
  • A monthly budget of up to $10,000 USD, which Google sets at $329 a day
  • An account run on the standard Google Ads platform
  • Additional Google Ads available through a separate account
Who Qualifies

Check your
fit in
one minute.

  • A US organization recognized by the IRS under Section 501(c)(3), or a group exempt organization affiliated with a central 501(c)(3)
  • Verified as a nonprofit through Google for Nonprofits and its validation partner Goodstack
  • A website on a domain your organization owns, served over HTTPS
  • A clear mission and original content on that website
  • An organization operated exclusively for charitable purposes
Google lists these as ineligible
  • Governmental entities and organizations
  • Hospitals and health care organizations
  • Schools, academic institutions and universities

Charitable arms and foundations of healthcare organizations are eligible. So are philanthropic arms of educational organizations. Fiscally sponsored organizations without their own 501(c)(3) are not eligible today.

If your organization does not qualify, we tell you in the first conversation. Google decides every application.

Monthly Rules

The grant funds the ads.
Keeping it active
takes monthly work.

Google attaches performance and structure rules to every Ad Grants account. These are the rules that apply each month, taken from Google's own policy pages.

5%
Click-through rate

Google requires a 5% click-through rate each month, measured across the account. Two consecutive months below 5% lead to temporary deactivation.

1+
Tracked conversion

At least one meaningful conversion each month. Donations, volunteer sign-ups, email sign-ups, information requests and calls count.

2+
Ad groups and sitelinks

At least two ad groups in every campaign, at least two sitelinks and specific location targeting.

Q3
Keyword quality

Keywords with a Quality Score of 1 or 2 are paused or removed. Single-word keywords and overly generic keywords need Google's exceptions or removal.

SB
Smart Bidding

Accounts created on or after April 22, 2019 use conversion-based Smart Bidding, unless they run Smart campaigns.

1x
Annual survey

Every grantee completes Google's annual program survey.

Google states that any account found in violation of program policies is subject to automatic suspension without notification. We review your account every month and fix drift early.

Questions

Straight answers
about Ad Grants.

What is Google Ad Grants?

Google Ad Grants gives eligible nonprofits up to $10,000 a month in Search advertising on Google.com. Your ads appear when someone searches for what your organization does. Google sets the monthly budget at $10,000, which equals $329 a day, and treats it as a limit. Actual spend depends on keywords, bids, ad quality and search demand.

Does our nonprofit qualify?

A US organization qualifies when the IRS recognizes it under Section 501(c)(3), or when it holds group exempt status through a central 501(c)(3), and when Google's partner Goodstack verifies it as a nonprofit. Governmental entities, hospitals and health care organizations, and schools, academic institutions and universities are not eligible. Charitable arms of healthcare organizations and philanthropic arms of educational organizations can qualify. Fiscally sponsored organizations without their own 501(c)(3) are not eligible today. Google decides every application, and we check your fit in the first conversation.

Does the grant cost anything?

Google funds the ad spend through the grant. Loveland Brand Design prices its management as fixed scope and fixed fee, with no hourly billing, and we state the fee before you sign anything.

What does Loveland Brand Design do for Ad Grants?

We apply, set up and manage. We prepare the Google for Nonprofits verification and the Ad Grants activation request and walk your administrator through each step Google requires. We set up conversion tracking, the account structure, keywords, ads and location targeting. Each month we review click-through rate and conversions, clean up keywords, test ads, complete the annual program survey and send a report.

Why does the account need monthly management?

The grant carries rules that apply every month. Google requires a 5% click-through rate each month, at least one tracked conversion each month, at least two ad groups per campaign, at least two sitelinks, specific location targeting and conversion-based Smart Bidding. Keywords with a Quality Score of 1 or 2 must be paused or removed, and the account must complete Google's annual program survey.

How long until ads run?

Google states that most Goodstack verification requests are reviewed within 3 to 5 business days and that the Ad Grants activation review may take several business days. After approval, Google sends an invitation email that can take up to 3 business days to arrive. Google controls both reviews, so we give you the steps and the order, and we report each Google decision as it arrives.

What happens if the account falls out of compliance?

Google states that two consecutive months below the 5% click-through rate lead to temporary deactivation until the account is corrected and reinstated. Google also states that any account in violation of program policies is subject to automatic suspension without notification. We review the account every month so drift gets fixed early.

Does our website need to change?

Google reviews the website before it approves activation. Your organization must own the domain, the site must use HTTPS across every page, and the submitted domain cannot redirect elsewhere. The site needs a clear mission, substantial and unique content, working navigation, fast and mobile friendly pages, and working donation links, with no AdSense. When the site falls short, we build or repair it through our Digital Ecosystem work before the account launches.
